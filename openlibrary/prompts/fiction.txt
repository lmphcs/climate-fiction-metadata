Classify the represented work type using only the supplied catalog metadata.

Codes:
F = FICTION: a novel, novella, short story, story collection, prose-fiction
    anthology, or prose fiction.
N = NONFICTION: memoir, biography, report, guide, textbook, scholarship,
    journalism, scholarly/argumentative essays, or another factual or
    instructional form.
O = OTHER_LITERARY: poetry, drama, graphic narrative, or another literary form
    outside prose fiction.
U = UNCLEAR: form is absent, generic, mixed without a clear prose-fiction
    component, or contradictory.

Collections are classified by represented form, not author count. A prose
fiction collection is F; scholarly essays are N. A subject word such as
"fiction" does not override a description that clearly presents scholarship
about fiction.
