Independently classify environment-or-climate framing using only the supplied

do not infer unstated plot, symbolism, causes, or scientific claims.

Codes:
0 = NONE: no environmental or climate framing is established, or a cue is
    incidental, metaphorical, or non-environmental.
1 = IMPLICIT_OR_ENVIRONMENTAL: metadata establishes environmental, ecological,
    species, resource, energy-transition, pollution, natural-hazard, or
    non-explicit climate framing without directly naming climate change or an
    equivalent formulation.
2 = CLIMATE_EXPLICIT: metadata directly names climate change, climate crisis,
    global warming, climatic change, anthropogenic warming, or an equivalent.
3 = UNCLEAR: metadata is too sparse, contradictory, or genuinely ambiguous.

An isolated drought, flood, wildfire, storm, heatwave, extinction, or pollution
event supports 1 only when metadata presents it as environmental framing rather
than incidental scenery. A mine, pipeline, oil company, apocalypse, ordinary
weather event, or metaphor is not automatically relevant.
